The agent found my citations. Then it found my mistakes
13 September 2026 · 7 min read

I stripped the citations out of my own research document and asked an agent to put them back, with a quoted passage for each. It got all ten right and found fourteen more I had missed — and the one mistake it made was the one kind my audit could never have caught.
